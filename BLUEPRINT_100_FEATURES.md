# AutoZap Platform: 100 Features Blueprint 🚀

This blueprint outlines 100 features to transform AutoZap from a simple booking tool into a comprehensive Salon & Business Management Ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Category 1: Core Operations & Management (25 Features)
1.  **Multi-Salon Dashboard:** Single login to manage multiple salon branches.
2.  **Seat/Chair Management:** Real-time status of every chair (Available, Cleaning, Occupied).
3.  **Advanced Staff Scheduling:** Shift management with lunch break and leave tracking.
4.  **Staff Commission Engine:** Automatic calculation based on services performed.
5.  **Inventory Management:** Track usage of shampoos, oils, and products.
6.  **Low Stock Alerts:** Automatic notifications when products are running low.
7.  **Supplier Management:** Directory of suppliers with one-click ordering.
8.  **Service Bundling:** Create packages (e.g., Haircut + Shave + Facial for a discount).
9.  **Walk-in Entry:** Quick interface for adding customers who didn't book via WhatsApp.
10. **Digital Receipts:** Send PDF receipts via WhatsApp after service completion.
11. **Waitlist Management:** If fully booked, add customers to a "First Available" list.
12. **Equipment Maintenance Tracker:** Reminders for clipper sharpening or AC servicing.
13. **Staff Attendance (QR Code):** Staff scan a QR code to clock in/out.
14. **Customer Profile History:** View past styles and preferences for personalized service.
15. **Product Upselling:** Suggest retail products during the booking flow.
16. **Expense Categorization:** Group expenses (Rent, Utilities, Marketing, Tea/Snacks).
17. **Petty Cash Management:** Track small daily cash outflows.
18. **Salon Occupancy Heatmap:** See which hours/days are busiest.
19. **Role-based Permissions:** Different access for Owners, Managers, and Staff.
20. **Audit Logs:** Track who changed what in the settings.
21. **Multi-Currency Support:** For international expansion.
22. **Service Duration Customization:** Different times for different hair lengths.
23. **Staff Skill Mapping:** Only allow bookings for services the staff member is trained for.
24. **Salon Photo Gallery:** Show shop interior and work samples in the bot.
25. **Integrated Calendar View:** Drag-and-drop booking management.

## 📈 Category 2: Growth & Marketing (25 Features)
26. **Loyalty Points System:** Earn points for every Rs spent; redeem for free services.
27. **Referral Program:** Give a discount to customers who bring friends.
28. **Automated "Miss You" Messages:** Notify customers who haven't visited in 30 days.
29. **Birthday/Anniversary Specials:** Auto-send discount codes on special days.
30. **Bulk Broadcast Tool:** Send festive greetings or limited-time offers.
31. **Google Maps Review Link:** Automatically send a review link after a positive visit.
32. **Social Media Integration:** Link Instagram/Facebook portfolios in the bot.
33. **First-Visit Discount:** Auto-apply a discount for new numbers.
34. **VIP Membership Tiers:** Bronze, Silver, Gold levels with exclusive perks.
35. **Cross-Promotion Engine:** Partner with local shops for joint vouchers.
36. **Peak Hour Pricing:** Dynamic pricing for weekends vs. weekdays.
37. **Happy Hour Discounts:** Lower prices during slow hours (e.g., 12 PM - 3 PM).
38. **Gift Cards:** Purchase digital vouchers for friends/family.
39. **In-Bot Feedback Surveys:** Simple 1-5 star rating after every visit.
40. **Customer Segmentation:** Group customers (Regulars, High-Spenders, Inactive).
41. **Seasonal Campaigns:** Pre-built templates for Eid, Diwali, New Year.
42. **SMS Backup:** Fallback to SMS if WhatsApp is down or blocked.
43. **Custom Bot Personality:** Change bot tone (Formal, Friendly, Local Slang).
44. **Affiliate Marketing:** Track bookings from local influencers.
45. **Promotional Banners:** Show images of current deals in the bot menu.
46. **QR Code Flyers:** Personalized QR codes for physical marketing.
47. **Automated Follow-up:** "How was your haircut?" message after 2 hours.
48. **Lookbook Feature:** Let users browse hairstyles in the bot before booking.
49. **Event Bookings:** Special flow for weddings or group parties.
50. **Partner Dashboard:** Let local hotels/malls book for their guests.

## 🤖 Category 3: AI & Automation (25 Features)
51. **AI Booking Assistant:** Natural language understanding (e.g., "aj sham ko haircut chahiye").
52. **Smart Slot Suggestion:** Suggest the most efficient slot to minimize gaps.
53. **Automated Cancellation Handling:** Offer the freed slot to the waitlist immediately.
54. **AI-Driven Stock Prediction:** Predict when products will run out based on history.
55. **Sentiment Analysis:** Alert the owner if a customer sends an angry message.
56. **Voice Note Support:** Allow customers to book via voice notes (transcription).
57. **Photo-based Consultation:** Customers send hair photos; AI suggests services.
58. **Automated FAQ Responder:** Handle questions about pricing, location, or timing.
59. **Staff Performance Prediction:** Predict who will be the "Star of the Month."
60. **Revenue Forecasting:** Predict next month's earnings based on trends.
61. **No-Show Probability:** Flag customers likely to miss their appointment.
62. **Automated Dispute Resolution:** Simple flow for handling refund requests.
63. **Multi-Language AI:** Detect and respond in Urdu, English, Punjabi, etc.
64. **Smart Upsell:** "Since you're getting a haircut, would you like a facial for 20% off?"
65. **AI Schedule Optimizer:** Suggest shift changes to match peak demand.
66. **Automated Competitor Tracking:** Manual entry of local prices for comparison.
67. **Image Background Removal:** For staff portfolio photos.
68. **Chatbot Handover:** Seamlessly transfer bot chat to a human manager.
69. **Automated Payouts:** Calculate and draft staff payouts automatically.
70. **Product Recommendation Engine:** Suggest home-care products based on service.
71. **Smart Reminders:** Send a 1-hour "Leaving soon?" reminder with location.
72. **Voice-Activated Dashboard:** "Hey AutoZap, show me today's revenue."
73. **AI Style Recommender:** Suggest haircuts based on face shape (via photo).
74. **Automated Content Creator:** Generate social media posts from salon data.
75. **Fraud Detection:** Identify fake bookings or bot spam.

## 📊 Category 4: Analytics & BI (25 Features)
76. **Daily Executive Summary:** Daily WhatsApp report to the owner (Revenue, Bookings).
77. **Staff Efficiency Report:** Revenue generated per staff member.
78. **Service Popularity Chart:** Which services bring in the most money.
79. **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):** Identify your most valuable 1% customers.
80. **Churn Rate Monitoring:** Track how many customers are not returning.
81. **Revenue Leakage Alert:** Notify if bookings are cancelled but revenue is low.
82. **Marketing ROI Tracker:** See which campaign brought in the most bookings.
83. **Inventory Wastage Report:** Compare expected vs. actual product usage.
84. **Customer Demographic Insights:** (If data provided) Age/Gender trends.
85. **Salon Ranking:** Compare your branches if you have multiple.
86. **Time-to-Service Analysis:** How long does a "30 min" service actually take?
87. **Net Promoter Score (NPS):** Measure customer loyalty over time.
88. **Break-even Calculator:** See how many haircuts are needed to pay the rent.
89. **Tax Estimation Tool:** Automatic GST/Tax calculation for reports.
90. **Cash vs. Digital Split:** Ratio of payment methods.
91. **Repeat Customer Rate:** Percentage of customers coming back.
92. **Average Order Value (AOV):** How much does a customer spend per visit?
93. **Slot Utilization Rate:** How many hours were "empty" today.
94. **Staff Commission Projections:** Help staff see potential earnings.
95. **Historical Comparison:** This Eid vs. Last Eid performance.
96. **Export to Excel/PDF:** Comprehensive data exports for accounting.
97. **Live Activity Stream:** Real-time feed of bookings and payments.
98. **Customer Feedback Word Cloud:** See what words people use most in reviews.
99. **Operational Risk Dashboard:** Flag expired licenses or overdue maintenance.
100. **Public Performance Badge:** "Top Rated in [Area]" digital badge for social media.
